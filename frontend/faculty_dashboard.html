Faculty Guidelines

When creating or editing an exam, upload your syllabus file (.txt, .pdf, or .docx) and choose the count. The system parses it and generates matching multiple-choice questions automatically.

Once the exam is published, click the "Share" button to copy the direct student-exam link. Students can open this link to log in and start the exam instantly.
